Who is out-throwing their shape?

Filter the pitcher-season population by year, age, pitch type, handedness, and volume. Every row is scored by the same validated model.

The model is fixed, not refit. Shape expectation and edge were fit once on complete seasons (2015–2025), out-of-fold and grouped by pitcher, then applied to every row. Filtering changes which rows you see, never what the model learned — otherwise each filtered view would be reporting a different, unvalidated model. Edge is what pitch shape predicts beyond ordinary regression to the mean: positive means the shape argues for better results than a simple mean-reversion forecast would give.

Shape says / results say

Each point is one pitcher-season. The diagonal is parity. Above the line, results beat what the shape supports; below, the shape is better than the results. Distance from the line is the part that partly reverses.
